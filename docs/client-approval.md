## Services and audience

The six services are verified: accountancy, taxation, bookkeeping, payroll, VAT and company secretarial. The plain-English descriptions mention specific kinds of work that are typical of each service but have **not** been individually confirmed:

- **Accountancy:** preparing year-end accounts from client records, and explaining them.
- **Taxation:** Self Assessment tax returns; company tax affairs; explaining HMRC correspondence.
- **Bookkeeping:** ongoing bookkeeping, and catching up records that have fallen behind.
- **Payroll:** setting up and running payroll, payslips and reporting to HMRC.
- **VAT:** registration questions, preparing and submitting VAT returns, checking VAT records.
- **Company secretarial:** Companies House updates, confirmation statements, help with company formation and changes to directors or shareholders.

Audience wording: the site names "owner-managed companies, partnerships and sole traders" under small and medium-sized businesses. It also describes "professional firms" and "private clients" as client groups. Please confirm these are accurate.

The **Business accounts** page says the practice can prepare accounts appropriate to the business structure, help with bookkeeping, tax, VAT and Companies House requirements alongside, explain the accounts in plain English, and talk to people moving from another accountant. Please confirm each point.
