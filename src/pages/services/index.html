01

Accountancy

Accounts that meet your obligations and make sense to you.

Accountancy is the work of turning your business records into accounts: a clear statement of how the business performed over its financial year, and where it stands at the end of it.

Your accounts need to be accurate, but they should also be useful. We prepare them carefully and take the time to explain what they show, so the figures help you make decisions rather than simply being filed away.

It may help if you

  • run a limited company, partnership or sole-trader business
  • need year-end accounts prepared from your records
  • want to understand what your figures are telling you

02

Taxation

Help understanding and meeting your tax obligations.

What you need to report, and when, depends on how you earn your income and how your business is set up. We help businesses and individuals understand their tax obligations, prepare the right returns and keep track of the deadlines that apply to them.

Every situation is different, so we would rather talk your circumstances through with you than offer general rules. Tell us what you need and we will explain how we can help.

It may help if you

  • need to complete a Self Assessment tax return
  • run a company and want help with its tax affairs
  • have received a letter from HMRC and want it explained
Ask about tax

03

Bookkeeping

Accurate, up-to-date records that make everything else simpler.

Bookkeeping is the day-to-day recording of what your business earns and spends. When it is done well, year-end accounts, VAT returns and tax returns are quicker and more reliable, and you have a clearer picture of how the business is doing throughout the year.

We can help you keep your books up to date, or help bring them back into order if they have fallen behind.

It may help if you

  • keep records in spreadsheets, on paper or in a mix of places
  • would rather spend your time on the business than on data entry
  • know your books need catching up before a deadline
Ask about bookkeeping

04

Payroll

Support running payroll, from your first employee onwards.

Running payroll means more than paying wages. Each pay run involves working out deductions, providing payslips and keeping HMRC informed about what has been paid.

We can help you set up and run payroll so it is handled consistently, whether you employ one person or a larger team.

It may help if you

  • are taking on your first employee
  • want payroll handled alongside your accounts and tax
  • find payroll takes up more of your time than it should
Ask about payroll

05

VAT

Help with VAT registration questions, records and returns.

VAT affects how you price, invoice and keep records. Whether you are unsure if registration applies to your business or you are already submitting returns, we can help you understand where you stand and keep your VAT affairs in order.

Because we also help with bookkeeping, your VAT returns can be prepared from the same well-kept records.

It may help if you

  • are unsure whether your business needs to register for VAT
  • want help preparing and submitting VAT returns
  • would like your VAT records checked and kept in order
Ask about VAT

06

Company secretarial

Keeping your company’s statutory records and filings in order.

Running a limited company comes with administrative duties beyond the annual accounts, such as keeping the details held at Companies House up to date and filing a confirmation statement each year.

We can help you stay on top of these requirements, so your company’s official records remain accurate.

It may help if you

  • have recently set up, or are about to set up, a limited company
  • have changes to directors, shareholders or addresses to report
  • want company filings looked after alongside your accounts
Ask about company secretarial support
